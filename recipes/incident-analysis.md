# Recipe: Incident Analysis

Use for outage, degradation, error investigation, or recovery record.

## Suggested structure
1. Incident summary
2. Impact and time range
3. Observed symptoms
4. Timeline
5. Evidence
6. Confirmed cause, or hypotheses if not confirmed
7. Recovery actions
8. Verification after recovery
9. Follow-up / prevention

## Rules
- Do not promote a hypothesis to root cause without evidence.
- Separate "restored service" from "root cause fixed".
- Timestamp facts where timing matters.
- Motion is useful for symptom progression, queue buildup, propagation, and recovery.
